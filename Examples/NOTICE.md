# Sources and licences of the example corpora

GridPuzzle's code is licensed under the GNU AGPL v3.0 (see `LICENSE` at the
repository root). The puzzle files in this directory come from elsewhere and
keep their own terms.

- **CSP-Rules corpora: the `.clp` files.** They come from Denis Berthier's
  CSP-Rules-V2.1 distribution (https://github.com/denis-berthier/CSP-Rules-V2.1),
  which is licensed under the GNU General Public License v3. This repository's
  history begins with that distribution, and its examples were merged in
  December 2021. The files keep their original headers; their solving
  transcripts were later removed, as `README.txt` explains. They are
  redistributed under GPL-3.0, whose full text is in `COPYING-GPL-3.0.txt`
  next to this file.
- **Newspaper transcriptions: the `.pzl` files.** Files with `times` in their
  names were transcribed from puzzles published in The Times. The puzzles
  remain the copyright of their publisher, are kept only as solver test data,
  and are not licensed under this repository's licences. The one other `.pzl`
  file, `KillerSudoku/20201001_hard290.pzl`, has no recorded source; treat it
  the same way.
- **Scanner photographs in `BrowserScanner/Newspaper/`.** See the rights
  section of that directory's README.
